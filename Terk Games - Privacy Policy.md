# Terk Games — Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: October 23, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord bot "Terk Games" (the "Bot"), developed and operated by an independent developer ("we", "us", or "the Developer"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with your use of the Bot. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Bot and, where applicable, ask your server administrator to remove it from the server.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot processes information that is made available to it through the Discord API as a result of being added to a server, and information that is generated through the Bot's own features. This may include:

- Discord identifiers: server (guild) IDs and the text channel IDs designated by administrators for Steam and Epic Games announcements;
- A record, per server, of which free games have already been announced (game identifier, name, and platform), stored to avoid duplicate announcements;
- The server's configured timezone, used for timestamps in messages and logs;
- Basic technical logs, such as when the Bot joins or leaves a server and when administrator commands are run, used for administrative oversight and to diagnose issues.

The Bot does not collect payment information, government-issued identification, precise location data, or the content of users' messages, and it does not store personal information about individual Discord members. The game information the Bot announces is publicly available store data and is not personal information.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by the Bot is used solely to operate and provide its features, specifically to:

- Retrieve publicly available free-game listings from the Epic Games Store and Steam and post announcements in the channel(s) designated by the server administrator;
- Avoid announcing the same game more than once on the same server;
- Maintain server-specific configuration so the Bot behaves as intended on each server;
- Diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of the Bot.

We do not use collected information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade user information to third parties.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, information is processed on the basis of: (a) the legitimate interest of server administrators and the Developer in providing an automated free-game announcement utility; and (b) performance of the Bot's function as requested and configured by the server administrator who added it.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Data processed by the Bot is stored on the hosting infrastructure used to run the Bot, generally in structured data files associated with each server (identified by server ID). We take reasonable technical measures appropriate to a project of this scale to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Retention

Channel settings, timezone settings, and the record of announced games for a server are generally retained until changed or removed by an administrator, or until the associated server removes the Bot.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of data associated with you that is held by the Bot. Because the data the Bot stores is limited to server-level configuration and a record of announced games, and does not include personal information about individual members, there is typically no personal data associated with an individual member to access or delete. Server-level settings are managed by that server's administrators. You may also contact the Developer directly using the details in Section 11 with any questions or requests, including a request to delete data associated with your server.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates on top of the Discord API, retrieves publicly available listings from the Epic Games Store and Steam, and is hosted using third-party hosting infrastructure. These providers may process data as part of delivering their services, subject to their own privacy policies and security practices. The Developer does not control, and is not responsible for, the independent data practices of Discord Inc., Epic Games, Valve Corporation, or the Bot's hosting provider.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under the minimum age required to use Discord (currently 13 years old, or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from individuals who do not meet this requirement. If you believe a user under this age threshold has provided information to the Bot, please contact us so that we can address it.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Bot's features or applicable legal requirements. Material changes will be announced through the official support server or another reasonable means of communication. Continued use of the Bot after such changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
