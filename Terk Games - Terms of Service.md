## 1. Description of the Service

Terk Games is a Discord utility bot that automatically announces games that are currently free to claim on the Epic Games Store and Steam. Depending on how a server administrator configures it, the Bot may provide features including, but not limited to:

- Periodically checking publicly available Epic Games Store and Steam listings for games that are currently free to claim;
- Posting an announcement (including the game's name, original price where available, expiry date where available, a store link, and an image) in a channel designated by the server administrator for Epic Games and/or Steam, and avoiding repeat announcements of the same game on the same server;
- Commands allowing administrators to set or remove the Steam channel and the Epic Games channel for their server;
- Configuring the server's timezone, used for timestamps in the Bot's messages and logs;
